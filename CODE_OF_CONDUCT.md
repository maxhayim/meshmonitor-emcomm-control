# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and useful to the emergency-communications community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and technically constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on the software and communications workflow.
- Do not publish private radio, node, location, credential, personal, medical, or served-agency information without authorization.
- Clearly distinguish simulated exercise traffic from real operational traffic.
- Do not propose changes that allow remote/inbound activation of LIVE mode or simulated incident generation while LIVE.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, contributions, or participants that violate these expectations.
